What drives failed orders

All cancellations in Aug 2026 fall into one of four categories — each with a different action owner:

Restaurant
Did not accept / late confirmation / out-of-stock item → Cosmopolis (11), Pitești (5) are priority
Courier
Did not pick up on time, couldn't find the venue
Customer
Cancelled due to long wait, placed by mistake
Bolt / System
No courier found in zone — Pitești zone coverage issue
Cosmopolis — 11 cancellations in August (6.1% fail rate), up from 8 in July. This is the highest fail rate in the chain and must be investigated: likely a mix of late order acceptance and stock issues. Pitești — 5 cancellations (5.6%), with the slowest acceptance time in the chain (0.71 min). Both locations need an operational review call before September.
